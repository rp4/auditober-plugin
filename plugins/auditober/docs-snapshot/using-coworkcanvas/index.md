Your tenant's administrators configure the item types, fields, statuses, and
workflows you work with, so exact names vary from one organization to the next and
these guides use generic examples. What you see in the navigation, and on any
given item or step, depends on your [page access](https://docs.assureswarm.com/concepts/permissions/).

## Navigation

The navigation is built from your tenant's own data, so the middle of the row is
one entry per configured item type.
[Around the shell](https://docs.assureswarm.com/using-coworkcanvas/around-the-shell/) tours the whole frame,
including the avatar menu and the Activity Hub.

| Entry                                       | What it's for                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dashboards                                  | Widgets and reports summarizing items, workflows, and activity.                                            |
| Item type pages (for example Issues, Risks) | Browse, search, and filter the records of one item type. One page per item type configured in your tenant. |
| My Items                                    | Items you own, or that were shared with you.                                                               |
| Templates                                   | Reusable workflow templates you can start a new workflow from.                                             |
| Time                                        | Log and review hours against items, when your tenant has it enabled.                                       |
| Admin                                       | Tenant configuration. Visible only to administrators.                                                      |

Two entries people often go looking for are not in this row. **Workflows** is not
a top-level page: a workflow opens from its item or from a dashboard link. The
**forms inbox** sits in your avatar menu instead, except for external
collaborators, who get a **My Forms** entry in the navigation because it is most
of what they use.

:::note\[Default access]
An administrator decides which pages each person can open. The default grant
covers Dashboards, Templates, Time, and My Items; item type pages are granted one
at a time, which is why two colleagues often see different navigation. Admin is
granted separately, to administrators.
:::

## Forms

Forms assigned to you wait in your forms inbox, reachable from your avatar menu.
Open one, read the step context shown alongside it, answer the required questions
including any file uploads, and submit.
[Responding to forms](https://docs.assureswarm.com/using-coworkcanvas/responding-to-forms/) covers the whole
round trip, and [Forms](https://docs.assureswarm.com/concepts/forms/) explains how a form connects to the step
behind it.

## Time Keeping

**Time** in the navigation opens a weekly grid, Monday through Sunday, where you
log hours against the items you worked on. Entries move through `DRAFT`,
`SUBMITTED`, `APPROVED`, and `REJECTED`.
[Tracking time](https://docs.assureswarm.com/using-coworkcanvas/tracking-time/) has the grid, the limits, and
the review side.

## Working with agents

If your organization connects AI agents, they never write to your data directly.
Every change one proposes arrives as a suggestion for a person to approve or
reject, and [Reviewing suggestions](https://docs.assureswarm.com/using-coworkcanvas/reviewing-suggestions/) is
the procedure. To connect an assistant of your own, see
[Connect an agent](https://docs.assureswarm.com/mcp/connect/).
